Urban Heat Democratization · A public-interest climate observatory

Make heatvisible.Make actionpossible.

A public-interest workspace for seeing local heat patterns, understanding the evidence, and helping shape thoughtful action on cooling, shade, and public investment.

Explore a city

Urban Heat Democratization is an open, public-interest platform for understanding urban heat, cooling access, and transparent mitigation scenarios. It helps residents, educators, planners, and researchers inspect evidence, understand its limits, and discuss practical climate-resilience options.

Responsible use

Maps and scenarios support public conversation and local validation. They do not replace public-health guidance, engineering assessment, or community knowledge.

Frequently asked questions

What is Urban Heat Democratization?

It is an open, public-interest platform for exploring urban heat patterns, cooling access, source context, and transparent mitigation scenarios.

Are the scenario results predictions?

No. They are benchmark-based exploratory aids with visible assumptions, not calibrated city-specific engineering predictions.
