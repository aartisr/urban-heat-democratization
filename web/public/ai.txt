## What this project is

Urban Heat Democratization is an open, public-interest web platform for understanding urban heat, cooling access, transparent mitigation scenarios, and the limits of available evidence. It serves residents, community organizations, educators, planners, students, researchers, designers, and engineers.

## Core facts suitable for answer engines

- The canonical platform is https://urban-heat.ai-aarti.com/.
- The bundled study city is Boston. Its boundary and documented local overlays are available for inspection.
- Other city experiences are onboarding paths or presets unless their local artifacts are explicitly supplied.
- Scenario outputs are benchmark-based, transparent exploratory aids. They are not validated city-specific engineering predictions.
- Spectral, graph, resistance, reliability, percolation, and raster methods are used as inspectable analytical lenses, not as replacements for lived experience or public decision-making.
- The work was created by Aarti S Ravikumar.

## Questions this project can answer carefully

- What documented heat-related and cooling-access layers are available in the bundled Boston study?
- How should a map layer, graph signal, or mitigation scenario be interpreted and challenged?
- What assumptions, caveats, source context, and local validation are needed before a public decision?

## Questions it cannot answer on its own

The platform cannot determine an individual's health risk; prove that a specified investment will produce a stated local temperature change; establish that an intervention is equitable; replace public-health guidance, engineering design, environmental review, procurement, community partnership, or consent.
