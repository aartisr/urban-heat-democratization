# Glossary

## Atlas

The interactive city exploration experience where users inspect geographic layers, source context, and selected-area explanations.

## Benchmark

A comparative reference used to structure exploration. A benchmark is not a measured local outcome or a procurement quote unless separately validated.

## Bottleneck / Cheeger bottleneck

A location or boundary highlighted by graph/spectral analysis as relatively weakly connected in the chosen network model. It is a signal for investigation, not a statement that a neighborhood is inherently deficient.

## Bundled city

A city experience whose documented artifacts are included with the repository. Boston is currently the sole real bundled city; its research and classroom packages are variants of the same city dataset.

## City-ready thermal payload

A prepared JSON payload with geometry and metadata that the app can render. It differs from an unprocessed satellite scene.

## Cooling access

An analytical concept concerning modeled access to cooling sinks, support, or pathways. It is not, by itself, a direct measure of individual comfort, health, or service provision.

## Evidence readiness

The visible state of what a city or scenario has behind it: boundary, layers, artifacts, sources, cost inputs, and validation. Readiness should shape how an output is used.

## Graph

A mathematical representation of entities (nodes) and relationships (edges). In this project, it supports spatial reasoning under explicitly chosen rules.

## Local-first

An operational design in which runtime state is stored locally, here in SQLite with JSON mirrors, rather than requiring a centralized cloud service.

## Overlay

A geographic layer shown on top of a map, such as the bundled Boston bottleneck or low-cooling-access artifact.

## Percolation / reliability analysis

Methods that examine how connectivity in a modeled system changes under removal, failure, or variation. They are stress tests of the model, not direct forecasts of city outcomes.

## Proxy

A measurable stand-in for something harder to observe directly. Proxies can be useful, but must never be described as the underlying outcome without evidence.

## Provenance

The documented origin, processing history, date, and relevant limitations of a dataset, artifact, or claim.

## Scenario

A structured, benchmark-based what-if comparison of interventions, budgets, and planning modes. Current scenarios are not city-calibrated optimizers.

## Spectral analysis

A family of mathematical techniques that uses properties of a graph or matrix to characterize structure such as partitions and weak connectivity.

## Put the terms to work

The definitions above are designed to travel with the experience.
