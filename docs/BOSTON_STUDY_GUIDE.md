# Boston Study Guide

Boston is the flagship example city in Urban Heat Democratization.

This guide is meant for educators, planners, students, researchers, and community advocates who want one concrete city they can study end to end without guessing where to begin.

## Why Boston

Boston is the strongest real-data example currently bundled in this repo because it already has:

- a real boundary used by the app,
- a real exported Cheeger bottleneck overlay,
- a real exported low-cooling-access overlay,
- source-backed cost references,
- a Boston-first scenario workflow,
- a Boston-first run inspection workflow.

## What The Boston Layers Mean In Plain Language

- `Cheeger bottleneck`:
  places where the urban heat network looks pinched or weakly connected, so heat can get trapped and relief does not spread well.
- `Low cooling access`:
  places where people appear to have weaker access to cooling sinks or cooling support.
- `Spectral highlights`:
  summary numbers that describe how fractured or connected the city's cooling pathways look.

You can think of Boston as a city map with:

- red shapes for the hardest heat traps,
- blue shapes for the weakest cooling access,
- scenario tools that ask what we should fund first.

## Best Way To Study Boston In The App

1. Open the Boston city page.
2. Read the map before reading the numbers.
3. Look for red bottlenecks and blue low-access areas.
4. Open scenarios and test a small budget, a medium budget, and a stretch budget.
5. Queue a Boston run so the analysis leaves behind an audit trail.
6. Review the run detail page to see attached artifacts and event notes.

## What Is Real Today

- Boston boundary
- Boston bottleneck overlay
- Boston low-cooling-access overlay
- repo cost-source references
- Boston study workflow in the TanStack app

## What Is Still Not Fully Real Yet

- full Boston procurement-ready unit costs for each intervention
- city-specific heat-reduction estimates per action
- city-specific equity impact estimates per action
- a full Boston optimizer that can prove the best plan under a budget

## Honest Interpretation

Use Boston today as:

- a real example city for observation,
- a real example city for teaching,
- a real example city for artifact-backed planning discussion,
- a partially complete example city for budget scenario exploration.

Do not use Boston today as:

- a final engineering estimate,
- a procurement schedule,
- a guaranteed citywide cost model.
