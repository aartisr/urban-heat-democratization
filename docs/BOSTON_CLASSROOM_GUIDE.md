# Boston Classroom Guide

This guide is a teaching-first companion to Boston's real bundled heat artifacts.

## What students should notice

- The boundary file shows the real Boston study area used in the repo.
- The bottleneck overlay highlights places where heat gets trapped more easily.
- The low-cooling-access overlay highlights places where people may have weaker access to cooling support.

## A simple classroom sequence

1. Open the map and ask where the red and blue shapes cluster.
2. Compare a few zones and ask what neighborhood conditions might explain them.
3. Open a starter scenario and discuss how a limited budget changes priorities.
4. Review the implementation log to separate what is real local data from what is still benchmark planning.

## What is real and what is not

- The bundled Boston boundary and overlay files are real local artifacts in this repo.
- The scenario budget logic is still benchmark-based.
- The app does not yet claim a validated Boston-specific benefit model for temperature or equity outcomes.
